<h2>ISO/TC 204 — Intelligent Transport Systems</h2><p>The <strong>ISO/TC 204</strong> vocabulary contains terminology definitions for intelligent transport systems (ITS) as defined in <strong>ISO 14812</strong>. It covers concepts related to connected and automated driving, traffic management, vehicle-to-infrastructure communication, and transportation data models.</p><h2>Content</h2><p>This dataset contains 312 concepts with English-language definitions.</p><h2>Source</h2><p>Maintained by ISO Technical Committee 204 (Intelligent transport systems). Definitions follow the Glossarist concept model aligned with ISO 10241-1, ISO 30042 (TBX), ISO 25964/SKOS, and ISO 12620.</p>
