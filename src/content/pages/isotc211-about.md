<h2>ISO/TC 211 — Geographic Information / Geomatics</h2><p>The <strong>ISO/TC 211 Multi-Lingual Glossary</strong> is a comprehensive terminology resource for geographic information standards. It provides definitions in 15 languages, covering concepts from ISO 19100 series standards for geographic information, including spatial referencing, data quality, metadata, and web services.</p><h2>Content</h2><p>This dataset contains 1,302 concepts with multilingual terminology across 15 languages: English, Arabic, Chinese, Danish, Dutch, Finnish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Polish, Russian, Spanish, and Swedish.</p><h2>Source</h2><p>Maintained by ISO Technical Committee 211 (Geographic information/Geomatics). Definitions follow the Glossarist concept model aligned with ISO 10241-1, ISO 30042 (TBX), ISO 25964/SKOS, and ISO 12620.</p>
